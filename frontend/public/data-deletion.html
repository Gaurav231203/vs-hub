GDPR & Meta Compliance

User Data Deletion Instructions

App Name: VS Adda | App ID: 1700460381501081

In accordance with Meta Platform Policy and global data protection regulations (GDPR/CCPA), VS Adda provides a transparent and automated process for users to request complete deletion of their stored data.

Option 1: Disconnect via VS Adda Dashboard

  1. Log in to your VS Adda workspace.
  2. Navigate to Channels from the left navigation bar.
  3. Locate the social account you wish to remove and click the three-dot menu ➔ Disconnect Channel.
  4. Confirm deletion. All stored access tokens, channel metadata, and associated cached metrics will be permanently deleted from our database immediately.

Option 2: Revoke Access via Facebook / Instagram

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile ➔ Settings & Privacy ➔ Settings.
  2. Click on Apps and Websites.
  3. Find VS Adda and click Remove.
  4. Meta will automatically trigger our automated Deauthorize & Data Deletion Webhook:
    POST https://vs-hub-1.onrender.com/api/v1/oauth/meta/data-deletion

Option 3: Manual Data Deletion Request

You can also send a data deletion request directly to our privacy officer at privacy@vsadda.com or contact@vsadda.com with your account email and brand name.
