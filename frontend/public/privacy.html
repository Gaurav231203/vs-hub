Privacy Policy for VS Adda

Effective Date: November 5, 2026 | App Name: VS Adda | App ID: 1700460381501081

1. Overview

VS Adda ("we", "our", or "us") provides a unified social media management, scheduling, and analytics workspace. This Privacy Policy details how we access, collect, process, and secure user and client data when connecting social networks including Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, and Pinterest.

2. Data We Collect & Permissions

  • Account Identifiers: Public page/profile names, handles, account IDs, and public profile images.
  • OAuth Access Tokens: Encrypted tokens used strictly to schedule and publish authorized media posts and retrieve engagement statistics.
  • Performance Metrics: Post impressions, reach, likes, comments, and engagement totals.

3. How We Use Data

Data retrieved from connected social media platforms is used exclusively to facilitate content scheduling, automated publishing, and analytics dashboard aggregation within your organization. We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or account information.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All sensitive credentials and OAuth access tokens are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption. Communication is strictly conducted over HTTPS/TLS 1.3.

5. User Data Deletion

Users may disconnect accounts or request full deletion of their data at any time by visiting our Data Deletion Instructions page or contacting support at contact@vsadda.com.

6. Contact Us

For inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please email contact@vsadda.com.
